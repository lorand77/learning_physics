Uniform motion in 1D

x(t) = x₀ + v·t. Press play, drag the time slider, or change x₀ and v and see how the motion and both graphs respond.

The motion

Faint dots mark where the car was at each whole second. They are evenly spaced because the velocity is constant.

Position vs time

A straight line. Where it starts on the vertical axis is x₀, and its slope is v.

Velocity vs time

A flat line, since v never changes. The shaded area is the displacement Δx = v·t.
