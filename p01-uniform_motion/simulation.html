Simulating uniform motion

A computer doesn't solve x(t) = x₀ + v·t directly. It moves time forward in small steps of Δt, and on each step it adds v·Δt to the position. Step through it yourself, then make Δt smaller.

The motion

Each dot is the position after one step. With constant v every step adds the same distance, so the dots are evenly spaced.

Position vs time

Each step goes Δt to the right and v·Δt up (orange). The dashed line is the exact solution x₀ + v·t. For uniform motion the steps land exactly on it, whatever Δt is.
